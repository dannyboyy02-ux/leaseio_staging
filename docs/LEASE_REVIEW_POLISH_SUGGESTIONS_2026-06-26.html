Headline — 4 CRITICALs, two of them defeat the product's core promise:
  • You can approve a lease you never reviewed. Confirmation state bleeds between leases (no per-lease reset), so opening a confirmed lease then navigating to an unreviewed one shows it "Ready to Approve."
  • Unlocking to edit rent/options strands you read-only — the two fields people most often unlock for stay locked.
  • The workbench can't scroll on a normal laptop (fixed-height shell + triple-nested scroll), and the PDF/form split never goes responsive — two cramped half-panes on a tablet.
  • Two more from a deeper felt-journey pass: unsaved edits vanish when you click the sidebar (no in-app nav guard — beforeunload only catches browser close), and the "show me where in the lease this came from" source-verify gesture is a 12px unlabeled sparkle almost nobody will discover.
What's already strong — don't touch. The deeper pass also confirmed the floor is high in places: the status strip is genuinely well-built — live progress ("X of 7 confirmed"), it names the remaining sections inline, offers a bulk "mark all" action, and the primary button intelligently flips to "Review N flagged fields." The "what do I do next?" question is largely answered. PDF source-linking exists (just under-surfaced — A-row below) and a dirty-state Save guard exists (just incomplete for in-app nav — A-row below). Everything below is polish on a solid base, not a rebuild.
